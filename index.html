How it works

Work first. Then play, on a budget.

Most screen-time limits are free minutes you can dismiss. FocusBank turns them into something you earn, so finishing your work is what opens your apps.

Add a commitment

Give it a deadline, a priority and a reward. High-priority tasks earn 30 minutes by default, medium 20, low 10. You can also set a penalty for missing it.

Finish it on time

The reward goes into your Vault. Finish a full day early and you also earn a reroll, which cancels your next penalty.

Spend your minutes

Opening a protected app counts down your Vault second by second. When it reaches zero, the app is blocked until you earn more.

Missed a deadline? FocusBank rolls the dice and locks one of your protected apps for the task's penalty time. The lock counts down while your Vault has time and pauses when it's empty. If the task is still unfinished when it ends, another app gets locked.

Features

Built to be hard to cheat, easy to live with.

Tasks

Commitments and plans

Commitments earn rewards and carry penalties. Plans are for scheduling only: a reminder, no reward, and they never keep your apps locked.

Limits

Daily time limit per app

Cap any protected app, say 30 minutes of Instagram a day. It still uses Vault time and is blocked when either the limit or the Vault runs out.

Unlock

Finish everything, use freely

Complete every commitment due today and your apps open for the rest of the day, even with an empty Vault. Your per-app limits still apply.

Wellbeing

Screen-free goal

Set minutes you want away from your phone each day. The daily unlock waits until you've reached them. Sleep doesn't count.

Planning

Week at a glance

Seven days in three-hour windows, colour-coded by priority. Sort tasks by due date, priority or the Eisenhower matrix.

Blocking

A block screen that helps

When an app is locked, FocusBank says why, shows the countdown, and points you to the most important task you could finish next.

Privacy

Your data never leaves your phone.

FocusBank has no server and no account. It doesn't even request internet access.

Read the privacy policy

  • Only what blocking needs
    The Accessibility service sees which app is on screen and whether the screen is on. It doesn't read what you type or what other apps show.
  • You decide first
    An in-app explanation asks for your consent before you enable anything, and you can turn it off any time in Settings.
  • Kept out of cloud backup
    Your tasks and usage stay on the device. Export a backup file yourself if you want one.
  • No ads, analytics or trackers
    Nothing is collected, sold or shared.

Under the hood

How it's built, and why.

FocusBank is a native Android app written by one developer. Every tool was picked to keep it offline, fast to react and simple to maintain.

Kotlin + Compose

Native, not cross-platform

Blocking apps is only possible on Android, and the core (Accessibility, alarms, the block screen) has to be native Kotlin anyway. Jetpack Compose redraws the countdown every second with far less code than XML layouts.

Accessibility

Reacts the moment an app opens

Chosen over usage statistics, which report only after the fact and can't block. A VPN would block only network traffic, and device-owner mode needs a factory reset.

Room + preferences

All data stays on the phone

Room (SQLite) keeps tasks and daily usage, with checked queries and safe upgrades. The balance, which changes every second, lives in fast built-in preferences. A cloud database was never an option: the app has no internet permission.

AlarmManager

Deadlines that fire on time

Deadlines belong to a clock time, so they use alarms that still run in battery-saving Doze mode. Background-job schedulers were ruled out because they decide for themselves when to run.

89 tests

Tested without a phone

The rules and the blocking engine run under JUnit and Robolectric on a laptop in seconds, using a fake phone with a scripted clock. Phone-maker quirks are checked on a real device.

GitHub

Free, signed and versioned

Every release is signed with the same key, so updates install over the old version and keep your data. GitHub Releases hosts each version with its checksum, and this site is plain HTML on GitHub Pages, with no trackers.

Questions

Good to know

Is FocusBank free?

Yes. There are no ads, subscriptions or in-app purchases.

Why does it need Accessibility permission?

It's the only way an Android app can tell, the moment you switch apps, that a protected app has opened, and then show the block screen. FocusBank only uses the app's package name and whether the screen is on.

Can't I just turn it off?

Yes, in Settings, and that's deliberate. FocusBank is a self-control tool for your own phone, not parental control. It makes turning it off a conscious choice rather than a single tap inside the app.

Will it drain my battery?

It reacts to app switches and runs one light check per second while it's on. It needs no network and does no background syncing.

Will it come to Google Play?

That's the plan. When it does, it will be a separate install: back up your data from the Vault screen first, then install the Play version.

Something isn't working

Some phone makers stop background services to save battery. If blocking stops, check that FocusBank is still on in Accessibility settings and allowed to run in the background. You can report problems on GitHub.
