2. What FocusBank is

FocusBank is a personal self-control tool. You choose which apps to protect, set your own tasks, rewards, penalties and goals, and the app limits your own use of the apps you selected. It works on an honour system: the rules are ones you set for yourself, and you can change them or turn the app off.

3. Using it responsibly

Use FocusBank only on your own device and for yourself. Do not protect apps you may need in an emergency, such as the phone dialer, messaging you rely on for safety, maps or health apps. You can always stop blocking by turning FocusBank off in Settings › Accessibility, or by uninstalling it. FocusBank is not a parental-control or monitoring tool and must not be used to watch or restrict another person.

4. Accessibility service and permissions

Blocking only works if you turn on FocusBank's Accessibility service. While it is on, FocusBank sees the package name of the app on screen and whether the screen is on or off. It uses this only to count your allowance, apply penalties, track screen-free time and show the block screen. It does not read what you type or the content of other apps. Notification permission is used for reminders, penalties and the ongoing protection notice.
